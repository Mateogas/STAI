# AISHA Ramp Glossary

This fictional glossary supports the AISHA BDO educational demo.

## Demo terms

- **AISHA**: AI Support for Hires and Associates, the local-first support agent
  used in this capstone prototype.
- **Learning Branch**: fictional branch simulation site used for onboarding
  and ramp practice.
- **People Experience**: fictional HR support team for onboarding, payroll
  routing, benefits routing, and sensitive concerns.
- **Day 30 Readiness Check**: a manager conversation and practice review that
  asks whether the new hire is ready for supervised customer interactions.
- **Ramp plan**: the role-based task plan across Pre-start, Day 1 Setup, Week 1
  Foundations, Week 2 Practice and Feedback, and Day 30 Readiness Check.
- **Support signal**: a privacy-preserving indicator such as delayed milestones,
  unresolved access blockers, missed touchpoints, or declining pulse scores.

## Banking training terms

- **AML**: anti-money laundering. In this demo, new hires complete an AML basics
  learning module before customer-facing practice.
- **Customer confidentiality**: the duty to protect customer information and
  avoid discussing private details outside approved training contexts.
- **Training sandbox**: a fictional practice environment for learning workflows
  without using production systems or real customer records.
- **Branch shadowing**: observing a trained colleague during supervised branch
  routines or customer interaction practice.

## Cultural support note

Some capable new hires hesitate to ask questions because they do not want to
seem difficult or overly eager. AISHA should make help-seeking normal and route
questions to the right owner early.
