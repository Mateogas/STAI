# Meridian Labs Jargon & Acronym Glossary

Every workplace speaks in shorthand and pretends everyone already knows it. This
glossary decodes both **Meridian-specific names** and **general workplace
jargon**. Hear something in a meeting that isn't here? Ask this assistant, ask
your buddy, or suggest an addition in #new-hires — someone else is wondering too.

## Meridian-specific names (you can't google these)

- **Meridian People** — our HR system: payslips, benefits enrollment, time off
  requests, your 30-60-90 plan of record.
- **The Hub** — the company intranet: org chart, equipment ordering, learning
  budget requests, policies.
- **Compass** — the monthly all-hands meeting, hosted by Elena Vasquez (CEO).
  First Thursday of the month, 16:00, streamed and recorded. New hires get
  welcomed by name at their first one.
- **Summit / Basecamp / Lookout** — the three big meeting rooms on Floor 4.
- **Northstar** — the CRM used by Sales (accounts, deals, pipeline).
- **Aurora** — shorthand for Aurora Health, our insurance provider.
- **Veritas** — the background-check vendor (see *Pre-Employment Requirements*).
- **Neighborhood** — the cluster of desks where a team sits (see *Office
  Logistics*).
- **#ask-people / #it-help / #workplace** — the Slack channels for HR, IT, and
  office questions. Public by design: your question helps the next person.

## People & process jargon

- **1:1 ("one-on-one")** — a recurring private meeting with your manager. Yours
  is weekly at first. It's your meeting: bring questions, blockers, career
  topics. Not a performance review.
- **PTO** — paid time off (vacation). **OOO** — out of office.
- **EOD / EOW** — end of day / end of week. "Send it by EOD" = today.
- **Async** — done in writing, on your own schedule, instead of a meeting.
- **OKRs** — Objectives & Key Results: quarterly company/team goals. Set in the
  first two weeks of each quarter; visible in The Hub.
- **KPI** — key performance indicator; a metric a team is judged by.
- **ERG** — employee resource group (e.g., Women@Meridian, Queeridian, Parents).
  Open to all; list in The Hub.
- **All-hands** — a meeting for the whole company (ours is Compass).
- **Offsite** — a work trip where a team plans/bonds for a few days.
- **Probation / intro period** — your first 90 days; the 30-60-90 plan is how we
  make it explicit rather than a mystery.
- **Onboarding buddy** — a teammate assigned to answer literally any question in
  your first 90 days.

## Engineering-flavored (heard everywhere anyway)

- **Standup** — daily ~10-minute team sync: what's done, what's next, blockers.
- **Sprint** — a 2-week block of planned work. **Retro** — the meeting where the
  team reflects on the sprint.
- **PR (pull request)** — a proposed code change others review. **LGTM** —
  "looks good to me," an approval.
- **CI/CD** — the automation that tests and ships code.
- **On-call** — carrying the pager for production issues for a week. Engineers
  shadow at day 60, own with backup at day 90.
- **RFC / design doc** — a written proposal circulated for feedback before
  building.
- **Ship** — release to users. "We shipped" = it's live.

## Sales-flavored

- **ICP** — ideal customer profile: who we sell to.
- **Discovery call** — the first call to understand a prospect's problem.
- **Pipeline** — all deals in progress, tracked in Northstar. **Quota** — a
  salesperson's revenue target. **Ramp** — the reduced-quota onboarding months.
- **ARR** — annual recurring revenue. **Churn** — customers leaving.
- **Demo cert** — internal certification to demo the product solo (day-30 sales
  plan item; Leo Tanaka runs it).

## Analytics-flavored

- **Warehouse** — the central analytics database.
- **Dashboard** — a self-updating page of charts answering a standing question.
- **Metrics dictionary** — the agreed definition of every company metric, in The
  Hub. When two dashboards disagree, the dictionary wins.
- **NPS** — net promoter score; a customer-satisfaction metric.
