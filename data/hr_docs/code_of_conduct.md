# Conduct and Support Expectations

This fictional conduct guide exists only for the AISHA BDO educational demo. It
does not describe official BDO policy.

## Working norms

- Treat customers, trainees, managers, buddies, and support teams with respect.
- Ask early when you are blocked. In this demo, asking for help is part of the
  ramp, not a negative signal.
- Protect customer and employee information. Do not discuss private details in
  open spaces or public channels.
- Use the demo systems only for training scenarios and fictional data.

## Escalation

Contact **Liza Villanueva** for sensitive concerns, workplace conduct questions,
or support needs that should not wait for the next manager check-in.

## AISHA privacy boundary

AISHA gives HR enough signal to offer help, not enough detail to police the
employee. HR support views should show delayed milestones, unresolved blockers,
missed touchpoints, concern tags, and suggested actions rather than raw private
chat transcripts by default.
