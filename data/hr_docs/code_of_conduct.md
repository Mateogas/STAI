# Meridian Labs Code of Conduct

This document applies to everyone at Meridian Labs — including leadership — in
the office, remote, at offsites, and in company channels. It is deliberately
short: it covers how we treat each other, how we handle information, and what to
do when something is wrong. The owner of this policy is **Marcus Webb (Head of
People)**.

## How we treat each other

- **Respect is non-negotiable.** Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or
  retaliation — based on anything (gender, race, religion, age, orientation,
  disability, background, seniority) — is grounds for discipline up to
  termination. This includes "jokes," exclusion from work conversations, and
  conduct at social events.
- **Judgment-free questions.** Nobody at Meridian gets mocked for asking a basic
  question. Seniors are expected to make juniors feel safe asking; that
  expectation is part of performance reviews here.
- **Disagree openly, decide once.** Challenge ideas hard before a decision;
  commit to the decision after it. Criticize ideas, never people.
- **Gifts & conflicts of interest.** Don't accept gifts above ~$50 from vendors
  or customers; disclose personal relationships that intersect with work
  decisions (hiring, purchasing, managing) to your manager or People Ops.

## Confidentiality & information handling

You signed a confidentiality agreement in *Pre-Employment Requirements*; in
practice it means:

- **Customer data** is only ever handled in approved systems (see *IT Setup
  Guide*). Never in personal accounts, never in screenshots shared externally,
  never in unapproved AI tools.
- **Company-internal information** (roadmaps, financials, salaries, incidents,
  unreleased features) doesn't leave the company — including to friends,
  recruiters, or social media.
- **Personal data of colleagues** (addresses, salaries, health info, HR matters)
  is confidential even inside the company. If your role gives you access, you
  use it strictly for that role.
- When unsure whether something is shareable: it isn't, until Grace Obi
  (Security & Compliance) or People Ops says otherwise.

## Social media & speaking publicly

Personal accounts are yours; just don't speak *for* Meridian (that's Marketing),
don't share confidential information, and be the same respectful person online
that this document expects offline. Conference talks about our work are
encouraged — run the abstract past your manager first.

## Raising a concern

Multiple channels, use whichever feels safest:

1. **Your manager** — the default for most issues.
2. **Marcus Webb (Head of People)** — directly, for anything involving your
   manager or anything sensitive: marcus.webb@meridianlabs.io.
3. **Anonymous line** — The Hub → "Speak Up" — goes to People Ops and the CEO's
   office; anonymous by design, replies via the portal.
4. **This assistant** can file an HR escalation ticket for you at any time.

**Anti-retaliation:** raising a concern in good faith can never legitimately be
held against you. Retaliation is itself a conduct violation and is treated as
seriously as the original issue. Reports are handled confidentially, shared only
with the people needed to resolve them, and you'll get an acknowledgement within
2 business days.

## Enforcement

People Ops investigates reports promptly and proportionately. Outcomes range
from a conversation, to formal warning, to termination. We do not do public
shaming; we also do not do quiet tolerance of serial bad behavior.
