# Pre-start Requirements

This fictional checklist supports the AISHA educational demo. It is not an
official BDO hiring or pre-employment process.

## Before Day 1 Setup

- Upload fictional pre-employment documents in the demo HR portal.
- Confirm your Learning Branch arrival details.
- Read the AISHA educational disclaimer and privacy note.
- Complete the pre-start survey.
- Save the names of your manager, buddy, People Experience contact, IT access
  contact, and payroll/benefits contact.
