# Pre-Employment Requirements at Meridian Labs

Welcome aboard! Before your first day at Meridian Labs, People Operations needs a
few documents and tasks completed. Everything below is submitted through
**Meridian People** (our HR system) using the login link in your offer email.
Questions at any point: email **people@meridianlabs.io** or message **#ask-people**
on Slack once your account is active.

## Documents to submit (at least 3 business days before your start date)

1. **Signed offer letter** — countersign electronically in Meridian People.
2. **Government-issued photo ID** — passport, driver's license, or national ID.
   Used for identity verification and your building badge.
3. **Tax withholding election form** — determines how much income tax is withheld
   from each paycheck. If you have never filled one of these out before, see the
   *Payslip Explainer* for what withholding actually means.
4. **Direct deposit details** — bank name, account number, and routing/branch code.
   Payroll (Anita Kapoor) cannot issue your first paycheck without this.
5. **Signed confidentiality & IP agreement (NDA)** — covers company and customer
   data. The *Code of Conduct* explains our confidentiality rules in plain language.
6. **Emergency contact** — one name and phone number.
7. **Proof of education or certification** — only if your offer letter lists it as
   a condition (most engineering and analyst roles: highest degree; sales roles:
   none required).

## Background check

Meridian Labs runs a standard background verification (identity, employment
history, criminal record where legally permitted) through our vendor, Veritas
Screening. You will receive an email from Veritas within 2 days of signing your
offer. The check normally completes in 3–5 business days. Your start date is
conditional on the check clearing. If anything on the report worries you, talk to
Marcus Webb (Head of People) — a flag is a conversation, not an automatic no.

## Before day one (no documents, just prep)

- **Complete the pre-start survey** in Meridian People — t-shirt size, dietary
  restrictions, preferred name and pronouns, and accessibility needs.
- **Watch for your IT provisioning email.** IT (Tomas Lindgren's team) creates
  your accounts the Friday before you start. See the *IT Setup Guide* for what
  happens on day one.
- **Plan your commute.** Office address, parking, and transit options are in
  *Office Logistics*. New hires start on Mondays; orientation begins at 9:30 AM.
- **You do NOT need to choose health benefits before you start.** You have 30
  days from your start date to enroll. The *Benefits Overview* and *Benefits 101
  Explainer* will walk you through it during week one.

## Common questions

**"My background check hasn't cleared and I start Monday."** Contact
people@meridianlabs.io immediately — we can usually still start you on schedule
with a signed attestation.

**"I don't have a bank account yet."** You can start without one, but add direct
deposit details within your first week to avoid a delayed first paycheck. Payroll
runs semi-monthly (15th and last business day) — see the *Payslip Explainer*.

**"Can I send documents by email instead of Meridian People?"** No — email is not
an approved channel for identity documents. This is a data-protection requirement
(see *Code of Conduct*, "Handling personal data").
