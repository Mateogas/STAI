# Leave Policy

All leave is requested in **Meridian People → Time Off** and approved by your
manager. Balances update in real time in Meridian People. Questions:
**#ask-people**. (New-hire tip: the *Jargon Glossary* decodes PTO/OOO and
friends.)

## Vacation (PTO)

- **20 days per year**, accruing at **1.67 days per month** from your start date.
  You can go slightly negative (up to 3 days) with manager approval, so a trip in
  your first months is possible.
- **Carryover:** up to **5 unused days** roll into the next year (use by March 31).
  The rest expires — take your vacation; unused PTO is not paid out except where
  law requires.
- **Requesting:** any amount, any reason — you don't owe anyone an explanation.
  Courtesy notice: about 2× the length of the leave in advance (a week off →
  two weeks' notice) for planning, not permission theater.
- **Blackout dates:** none company-wide; some teams (Sales at quarter-end,
  on-call weeks in Engineering) coordinate within the team.
- Set your Slack status and an email auto-reply when OOO. Nobody is expected to
  read messages on vacation — we mean it; managers are told to enforce it.

## Sick leave

- **10 days per year**, available in full from day one — **no doctor's note for
  the first 3 consecutive days**; a note is needed from day 4.
- Sick days cover you, and caring for a sick child or dependent partner.
- Mental-health days are sick days. You do not need to specify the illness —
  "I'm sick today" in Meridian People + a note to your manager is enough.
- Long illness beyond 10 days moves to paid extended sick leave via People Ops —
  talk to Marcus Webb's team; the EAP (see *Benefits Overview*) is also there
  for you.

## Company holidays

**11 fixed public holidays** per year (the office closes; the list for the
current year is pinned in Meridian People and The Hub), **plus** a company-wide
**winter shutdown** between December 24 and January 1 that does *not* count
against your PTO. If you must work a holiday (on-call), you get a replacement
day.

## Parental leave

- **16 weeks fully paid** for every new parent — birth, adoption, or foster —
  regardless of gender, usable within the first year.
- Birthing parents may add accrued PTO and sick leave on top.
- Flexible return: a 4-day-week ramp for the first month back is standard if you
  want it. Coordinate timing with your manager and People Ops at least 8 weeks
  ahead where possible.

## Other leave

- **Bereavement:** 5 paid days (immediate family), 2 days (extended family) —
  taken without paperwork; tell your manager.
- **Jury/civic duty:** paid, just forward the summons.
- **Moving day:** 1 paid day per year.
- **Volunteer day:** 1 paid day per year for a cause of your choice.
- **Unpaid leave / sabbaticals:** case-by-case after 12 months of tenure;
  4-week paid sabbatical at 5 years.

## New-hire specifics (asked every single onboarding)

- **"Can I take vacation during my first 90 days?"** Yes. Accrual applies, the
  3-day negative buffer exists, and using leave never counts against you.
  Pre-planned trips agreed during your offer are always honored — flag them to
  your manager in week one.
- **"Do holidays count against my PTO?"** No — holidays and winter shutdown are
  on top of your 20 days.
- **"I was sick during my vacation."** Days you can document as sick convert
  back to sick leave. Yes, really.
