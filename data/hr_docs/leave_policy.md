# Leave and Absence Guide

This fictional guide is part of the AISHA educational demo and is not an
official BDO leave policy.

## How leave works in the demo

- File planned leave in the demo HR portal.
- Tell your manager if leave affects a branch shadowing session, compliance
  module deadline, buddy touchpoint, or Day 30 Readiness Check.
- For urgent sickness or emergency absence, message your manager first and file
  the portal record when practical.

## Who can help

- Manager questions: Carlo Santos for branch banking hires or the listed team
  manager for other roles.
- Portal or balance questions: Noel Bautista.
- Sensitive or private concerns: Liza Villanueva.

## Privacy note

Future attendance integrations are outside the current prototype. In the AISHA
story, absence patterns would only be used as one support signal with strict
privacy controls, not as presence tracking or desk-time scoring.
