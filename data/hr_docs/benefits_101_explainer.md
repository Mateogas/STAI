# Benefits 101 Explainer

This fictional explainer is written for fresh graduates and early-career hires
using AISHA in the BDO educational demo.

## Common words

- **Premium**: the monthly cost of being covered by a benefit plan.
- **Deduction**: an amount taken from gross pay before the final net pay.
- **Dependent**: a spouse, child, or other eligible person covered under a plan.
- **Contribution**: money paid into a required or optional program.
- **Enrollment window**: the period when a new hire chooses benefits.

## What Alyssa should do in Week 1 Foundations

1. Open the demo HR portal.
2. Review the fictional benefits choices.
3. Ask Noel Bautista if a term is unclear.

## What AISHA can and cannot do

AISHA can explain basic terms, point to this explainer, and route the new hire
to the right fictional owner. AISHA cannot make benefit elections, collect
private documents, or provide official advice.
