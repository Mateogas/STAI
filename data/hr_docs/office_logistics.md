# Office Logistics

Everything about the physical office. Owner: **Priscilla Ang (Office & Workplace
Manager)** — reach her and the workplace team at **#workplace**.

## Address & building

**Meridian Labs — Meridian Tower, 500 Harbor Point Blvd, Floors 3–5.**
Reception is on the ground floor; Meridian's own front desk is on **Floor 4**.

- **Floor 3** — IT desk, quiet/focus zone, phone booths.
- **Floor 4** — reception, People Ops, the big meeting rooms (Summit, Basecamp,
  Lookout), event space where Compass happens.
- **Floor 5** — team neighborhoods (Engineering, Sales, Analytics), cafeteria,
  the roof terrace.

## Hours, badge & access

- Building open **06:00–22:00** weekdays with your badge; weekend access needs a
  one-time request in #workplace.
- Your **badge** is issued on day one (see *First Day Guide*) and also holds the
  24/7 IT emergency number on the back. Lost badge → #workplace immediately
  (security), replacement same day.
- **Guests** must be registered in The Hub → Workplace → Visitors and are your
  responsibility while on-site. Customer visits: book Summit or Basecamp.

## Hybrid schedule

Meridian is hybrid: **Tuesday–Thursday are anchor days** in the office; Monday
and Friday are your choice. Teams may agree extra in-office days during crunch
periods (e.g., launch weeks). Fully remote arrangements are exceptional and go
through your manager + People Ops. During your **first two weeks, aim to be in
the office daily** if you can — onboarding is dramatically easier in person.

## Getting here

- **Transit:** Harbor Point station (two blocks) — commuter rail and the M4/M7
  bus lines. A **pre-tax commuter deduction** for transit passes can be enabled
  in Meridian People → Benefits (it shows up on your payslip — see *Payslip
  Explainer*).
- **Parking:** basement garage, $8/day badge-billed, or a $120/month pass
  (also pre-tax). Spots are first-come; anchor days fill by 9:00.
- **Bikes:** free secure bike room on P1 with showers and lockers on Floor 3.
  Register your bike once in The Hub.

## Food & coffee

- **Cafeteria (Floor 5):** breakfast 08:00–10:00, lunch 11:30–14:00.
  Lunch is subsidized — a full meal runs $4–6. Dietary needs from your pre-start
  survey (vegetarian, halal, gluten-free) are always available.
- **Coffee:** proper machines on every floor; the Floor 5 barista bar runs
  Tue–Thu, 08:00–11:00.
- **Snack wall:** free fruit, nuts, and (contentious) one brand of licorice
  Priscilla refuses to discontinue.

## Desks & neighborhoods

Teams sit in **neighborhoods** (clusters of desks). You'll be shown yours on day
one. Desks are not fixed within the neighborhood — but people are creatures of
habit; when in doubt, ask before taking a desk with a monitor covered in
stickers. Standing desks: all of them. Extra equipment: The Hub → Workplace →
Equipment (see *IT Setup Guide* for what's auto-approved).

## Everything else

- **Mail & packages:** personal deliveries allowed — pickup shelf by Floor 4
  reception, you'll get a Slack ping.
- **Gym:** building gym on P1, free with badge, towels provided. (Your $50/month
  wellness stipend — *Benefits Overview* — covers external gyms too.)
- **Quiet rules:** Floor 3 focus zone = library rules; phone calls go to booths.
- **Pets:** dogs on Fridays, registered in The Hub, one per neighborhood per day
  (there is a rota; it is taken very seriously).
- **Sustainability:** building runs on renewable power; bins are three-stream —
  when in doubt, the signs above each station rule.
