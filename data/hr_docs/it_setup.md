# IT Access Guide

This is fictional training content for the AISHA BDO educational demo.

## Day 1 access checklist

1. Pick up the training laptop and badge at the Learning Branch IT Bar.
2. Set up email, collaboration chat, and MFA with **Irene Chua**.
3. Confirm access to the learning portal with **Mark Alcantara**.
4. Confirm whether the core banking training sandbox is needed for your role.
5. Report any access blocker to AISHA, Irene, or your manager before the end of
   Day 1 Setup.

## Common blockers

- MFA enrollment loop: ask Irene to reset the demo factor.
- Missing learning portal tile: ask Mark to refresh the fictional LMS profile.
- Training sandbox locked: ask Irene to confirm manager approval for access.
- Laptop issue: visit the IT Bar or message @irene.it in the demo chat.

## Security reminders

- Do not share passwords or MFA codes.
- Do not use real customer information in training scenarios.
- Complete the data privacy and AML learning modules during Week 1 Foundations.
