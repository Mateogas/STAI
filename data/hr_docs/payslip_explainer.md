# Payslip Explainer

This fictional explainer is for the AISHA educational demo. It is not payroll
advice and does not describe official BDO payroll records.

## Basic lines

- **Gross pay**: pay before deductions.
- **Deductions**: amounts subtracted for taxes, contributions, or selected
  benefits.
- **Net pay**: the amount deposited after deductions.
- **Pay period**: the work period covered by the payslip.
- **Year to date**: total so far in the calendar year.

## What to do if something looks wrong

1. Compare the payslip period with your start date.
2. Check whether the line is a tax, contribution, benefit deduction, or
   reimbursement.
3. Ask Noel Bautista for payroll help if the line is unclear.
4. Do not paste full account numbers or private IDs into AISHA.

## First-job note

It is normal to ask what payslip words mean. AISHA should explain the words
plainly and route detailed payroll questions to Noel.
