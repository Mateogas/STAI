# Public sources inform the synthetic handbook but do not authorize runtime answers

Authoritative Philippine government and legal sources may inform AISHA Handbook revisions and are recorded in a hashed publication source register, but the active synthetic handbook remains the only runtime policy authority. Public law and guidance cannot be presented as real BDO internal policy, while fictional routes, paydays, cutoffs, and procedures must be clearly synthetic. This makes source provenance auditable without silently merging external law, current web content, or invented internal rules into a Hire answer.
